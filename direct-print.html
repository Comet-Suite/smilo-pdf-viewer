Overview

On-device · No uploads

A complete PDF reader that runs entirely inside your browser. Documents are parsed locally — nothing is uploaded. Rendered pages are prepared in memory so scrolling never shows a blank page, and your reading position, bookmarks, comments, highlights, watermark and signature stay on this device.

The viewer has no server and no account. Everything you do stays on the machine it runs on.

  • Documents never leave the page. Files are read in the browser, so untrusted PDFs are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your work is stored locally. Bookmarks, comments, highlights and your signature are saved in this browser’s local storage, per document.
  • Links are yours to approve. External links prompt before opening, and risky schemes are refused outright.
  • No analytics. Nothing is measured, tracked or reported back.
